Know more about Online MBA Program at DY Patil University

DY Patil University, accredited with a prestigious NAAC A++ Grade, is known for delivering industry-focused higher education in India. The Online Master of Business Administration (MBA) offered by DY Patil is a UGC Approved Online MBA program designed to meet the demands of today’s fast-changing business environment.

Key Highlights: DY Patil Online MBA Program

  • 2-Year MBA Program (Online Mode): This 2-year postgraduate degree blends academic depth with practical exposure, making it a strong choice for learners seeking one of the Best Online MBA in India. Structured across four semesters, the program builds strong foundations in management, strategy, and business leadership. 

  • Ideal for Working Professionals: Designed as an Online MBA for working professionals, it offers flexibility without disrupting your career. 

  • Industry-Relevant Curriculum: The DY Patil Online MBA curriculum aligns with leading universities and focuses on real-world business applications. 

  • Flexible 100% Online Learning : Learn through live virtual classes, recorded lectures, and digital resources at your own pace. 

  • Among the Best Online MBA Programs in India : Strong academic credentials and flexible delivery place this program among the best options available in India.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours should I plan per week to study for this program?

For an Online MBA, most learners set aside time each week to attend sessions, watch recorded content, and complete assignments. Your exact time will vary by semester and your comfort with the subjects.

Is there a fee for obtaining the original degree certificate?

Yes, the fee for obtaining the original degree certificate is ₹4,000, payable to D. Y. Patil University, Navi Mumbai. The fee may be revised as per the university’s fee structure. Learners will receive an email on their registered email ID with the applicable charges and application process.

Are there live classes, or is everything recorded?

Online learning usually includes a mix of learning formats. Live sessions help with interaction, while recorded content helps you revise at your own pace.

How are exams conducted, and when do they happen?

DY Patil’s examination model is based on term-end semester examinations, meaning exams are held at the end of each semester.

What does the 70–30 evaluation pattern mean?

The semester mark split is 70% Term-end Semester Examination (TESE) and 30% Internal Assessment Evaluation (IAE).

When do I choose my MBA specialisation?

You indicate your specialisation preference in the Online MBA admission form. Which means, when you apply, clearly mention whether you are locking in their specialisation at the time of application or if you can confirm it later to avoid confusion.

Are exam fees included?

Yes, for Indian students paying semester-wise, the Online MBA fees for exams are included for the first attempt, and the re-appear exam fee is INR 300 per subject.

What is the refund or cancellation process?

Eligible refunds are processed within 45 days of the university's receipt of the written application and are issued by electronic fund transfer.

Is the Online MBA degree valid?

DY Patil’s Online MBA is offered under its UGC–DEB entitlement for online learning, and the university is NAAC A++ accredited (plus it lists AICTE under online learning approvals). As per UGC, degrees awarded through online mode by UGC-recognised institutions are treated as equivalent to the same degree offered in the regular mode. Hence, the Online MBA is recognised on par with a regular MBA.

What is the duration of the Online MBA at DY Patil University?

The duration of Online MBA is a 2-Year. The programme is structured to provide comprehensive management education through a fully digital format, allowing learners to complete academic requirements within two years as per the university’s prescribed curriculum and academic guidelines.

Is the Online MBA from DY Patil University UGC approved?

Yes, the programme is UGC Approved Online MBA Degree from a NAAC A++ Accredited University.

What are the eligibility criteria for Indian students applying to the Online MBA?

Indian students must have a minimum 40% in Class 10 (SSC) and 40% in Class 12 (HSC) from a recognized board OR 50% overall in a 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma. Additionally, graduation with minimum 40% overall from a recognized university is required under the 10+2+Graduation pathway. Lateral entry (10+3+Graduation) requires 50% in both Graduation and Polytechnic Diploma.

Can diploma holders apply for the Online MBA at DY Patil University Online?

Yes. Candidates with a 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma with 50% overall may apply under the specified pathways. For lateral entry (10+3+Graduation), applicants must have 50% overall in Graduation and 50% in the Polytechnic Diploma, as per the university’s eligibility norms.

What documents are required during the Online MBA admission process?

Applicants are required to submit their 10th mark sheet, 12th mark sheet, and Graduation mark sheets as part of the documentation process. These documents are verified by the university before confirming admission and granting access to the Learning Management System (LMS).

How can international students enroll in the Online MBA programme?

International students must ensure they meet the academic eligibility criteria, including a recognized 10th equivalent, 10+2 equivalent, and a minimum 3-year Bachelor’s degree. They must also obtain an AIU Equivalency Certificate for foreign qualifications. The application process follows the same three-step registration, documentation, and fee payment procedure.
